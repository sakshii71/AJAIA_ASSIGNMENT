Roster Accuracy Engine: Before vs. After Protection

Previous Behavior (Unchecked OCR)

  • ❌ Wrong-kid thumbnails: Suspected contributor to the ~30% unopened reels (cause split unknown, measure first).
  • ❌ No Coach Gate: Ambiguous digit swaps (16/61, 1/11) automatically published to family reels without warning.
  • ❌ Support Burden: Upset parents had to file manual tickets (M3: "daughter was so disappointed").
  • ❌ Renewal Risk: Ridgeline's own parent filed a mis-tag ticket (14 vs 4) while the club is pushing for livestreaming ahead of renewal.

With Accuracy Engine & Human-in-the-Loop

  • ✅ Withhold by default (design goal): Conservative withholding ensures unconfirmed clips never reach parents.
  • ✅ Target: under 45s review per game (unvalidated): Resolves high-leverage edge cases without volunteer burnout.
  • ✅ Parent Safety Veto: Instant "This isn't my kid" button immediately purges mistakes with zero friction.
  • ✅ Transparent Communication: In-app notices clarify why clips were held back, maintaining family trust.
